| **Cinnamon (Dalchini) powder** | 1 tsp | Supports insulin sensitivity |
| **Dry ginger (Sonth) powder** | ½ tsp | Anti-inflammatory |

### ⚠️ Deliberately EXCLUDED for Now
**Dates, figs (anjeer), raisins (kishmish), cashews, jaggery, sugar** — these are naturally high in sugar/fructose. At TG 483 they work against you. **Re-introduce small amounts only after triglycerides drop below 200.**

### Preparation
1. **Dry roast each item separately** on low flame (3–4 min) until aromatic — do NOT brown or burn (burnt nuts = oxidised fats)
2. Cool **completely** to room temperature (critical — warm nuts turn the powder pasty)
3. Grind in short pulses in a mixer. Stop before it becomes nut butter — aim for a coarse-fine powder
4. Store in an **airtight glass jar in the refrigerator** (nut oils go rancid at room temperature in Indian humidity). Use within 6 weeks

### Dosage & Usage
- **1 heaped teaspoon (10–12 g) ONCE daily** — not more. This is calorie-dense (~70 kcal/tsp)
- **Best with:** 1 glass warm **toned milk** at bedtime, OR mixed into your morning curd, OR sprinkled over dalia/oats
- **Bonus benefit:** Taking it with milk/curd is deliberate — the calcium binds oxalates in your gut, which actually **reduces** kidney-stone risk while you get the nut benefits
