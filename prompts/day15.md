# Day 15 Tutorial: Breaking a Large Business System into Clear Areas

Create a practical, beginner-friendly tutorial for a .NET Technical Architect interview.

## Goal

Teach me how to take a large business application and break it into clear business areas based on responsibility.

Do not focus on coding. Focus on how an architect thinks and makes decisions.

Use very simple English. Avoid unnecessary technical jargon. Explain every technical term in simple words before using it.

## Cover these topics

1. What is a business area?
2. Why should a large application be divided into business areas?
3. How to identify business areas from requirements
4. How to decide which functions belong together
5. How to identify responsibilities
6. How to identify dependencies between areas
7. How to avoid creating too many small areas
8. How to avoid putting unrelated responsibilities together
9. Difference between business area and technical component
10. How business areas can later become modules or services
11. When to keep areas inside one application
12. When to separate an area into an independent service

## Use one realistic example

Use an Enterprise Inventory and Order Management system with:

* Companies
* Users
* Products
* Customers
* Suppliers
* Sales Orders
* Purchases
* Inventory
* Payments
* Notifications
* Reporting
* Audit

Show how an architect would group these into sensible business areas.

For each area explain:

* What it is responsible for
* What data it owns
* What it should NOT own
* Which other areas it depends on
* Why it is separated from the others

## Include a decision exercise

Give me 10 situations where I must decide:

"Should these two functions belong to the same business area or different areas?"

For each question, first let me answer, then provide the correct answer with a simple explanation.

## Include an architecture exercise

Give me this problem:

"Design the business structure for a multi-company Inventory and Order Management System."

Ask me to identify:

* Business areas
* Responsibility of each area
* Main data owned by each area
* Dependencies
* Areas that should remain together
* Areas that may need to become separate later

Do NOT provide the answer immediately.

First give me the exercise.

Then provide a separate "Suggested Answer" section so I can compare my solution.

## Interview preparation

Add 10 Technical Architect interview questions related to business areas and system boundaries.

For each question provide:

* What the interviewer is testing
* A simple answer
* A stronger Architect-level answer
* One common mistake

## Final challenge

Create one 20-minute architecture challenge where I must break a real business system into business areas.

Give me the requirements, constraints, expected users, and important business rules.

Do not solve it immediately.

## Final takeaway

End the tutorial with:

"5 rules I should remember as a Technical Architect"

Keep the entire tutorial practical, interview-focused, and easy to understand.

Do not introduce unrelated topics such as advanced coding, design patterns, or detailed cloud services.
